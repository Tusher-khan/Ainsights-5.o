Natural language analytics

Ask in the words you already use. AInsight works out the measure, the grouping and the filters from your dataset's real columns.

Metadata-aware retrieval

Column descriptions, types and value samples are indexed and retrieved per question. Your data rows are never sent to a language model.

Text-to-SQL, checked twice

Generated SQL is parsed, security-validated and run on a read-only connection. Anything that is not an analytical read is rejected.

CSV and Excel analytics

Upload a file and it is profiled immediately: types, missing values, duplicates, distributions and questions worth asking.

Interactive visualizations

Charts are built from executed results only. The chart type follows the shape of the answer, and falls back to a table when nothing fits.

Validated results

Every answer carries its source: the dataset, the columns, the operation and the checks it passed. If the data cannot answer, AInsight says so.
